# Endoora Learner Twin Governance

## Rules

- Twin claims must link to approved evidence.
- Estimates are not hidden personality analysis.
- Learners can correct or reset relevant evidence.
- Sensitive psychological inference is prohibited.
- Snapshots remain readable after updates.

## Evidence Model

Evidence sources may include:
- placement
- assignments
- practice
- SRS activity

Raw conversations and AI outputs are not automatically treated as learner facts.
