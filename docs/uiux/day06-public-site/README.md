# Day 06 public-site visual direction

Status: accepted implementation reference for the Day 06 public website.

## Design system

- Persian-first, RTL by default, with a complete English counterpart.
- True white and pale cool-blue surfaces, deep navy text, cobalt primary actions, and teal learning accents.
- Quiet sticky header, generous editorial whitespace, and disciplined section rhythm.
- Code-native product previews use neutral states such as “awaiting assessment”; they never invent learner scores, dates, testimonials, activity, or outcomes.
- Primary homepage journey: free placement. Secondary journey: account registration/start learning.
- The learning loop is a connected rail on desktop and a vertical sequence at 360 px.
- Feature explanations use alternating editorial rows rather than a same-size card dump.
- Teacher marketplace content explains AI plus human review, verification, and least-privilege data access.
- Trust and limitation content stays visible before the FAQ and conversion section.
- Optional analytics consent is a slim bottom notice. Day 06 loads no third-party analytics script.

## Reference boards

- `hero-and-learning-loop.png`: hero, evidence-neutral Learner Twin preview, connected learning loop.
- `features-and-trust.png`: feature storytelling and AI/human trust treatment.
- `faq-waitlist-footer.png`: FAQ, early-access form, footer, and consent notice.
- `mobile-360.png`: 360 px stacking and touch-target reference.

The synthetic dates, JSON snippets, counts, and learner data visible in the concept boards are not approved product content. The implementation deliberately replaces them with neutral UI states.

## Copy lock

- No guaranteed IELTS, CEFR, learning, or employment outcome.
- No official-score language for AI estimates.
- No fake testimonial, teacher, support address, phone number, uptime claim, or learner metric.
- Legal pages remain visibly marked as drafts and use `noindex` until legal review.
