# Endoora Product Constitution

## Product

Endoora

## Motto

A new door to your English

https://endoora.ir

## Primary launch audience

Iranian English learners and teachers.

## Product mission

Endoora is a bilingual Persian/English AI-powered learning system that helps learners understand their current English ability, follow a personalized learning path, practise consistently, identify recurring mistakes, receive useful feedback, connect learning evidence with teachers, and measure progress over time.

## One-sentence Production V1

Endoora helps an English learner assess their current level, receive a personalized learning path and daily practice, preserve useful learning evidence, work with teachers, and track progress in one bilingual learning system.

## Product principles

### 1. Learning outcome before feature count

Endoora will not measure product quality by the number of available features.

Features must strengthen a meaningful learner, teacher, marketplace, content, IELTS, or business loop.

### 2. One obvious next action

Learner and teacher dashboards must prioritize the most useful next action instead of presenting every feature equally.

### 3. Explainable AI

Important AI recommendations must be connected to understandable evidence.

AI estimates must never be presented as official educational certification.

### 4. Evidence is not automatically truth

Raw conversations, writing, audio, exercises, and AI output do not automatically become permanent learner facts.

Structured evidence must pass the relevant validation or approval process.

### 5. Privacy and safety are product features

Learner data, writing, audio, teacher relationships, payments, and private educational evidence must receive server-side access control and appropriate retention rules.

### 6. Persian-first, English-aware

The default interface is Persian-first RTL.

English learning examples, IELTS passages, code, URLs, email addresses, IPA, and English answer fields remain correctly isolated as LTR content.

### 7. Calm rather than addictive

Gamification may encourage useful learning behavior but must not use manipulative engagement patterns.

### 8. Honest assessment

Endoora may provide provisional CEFR estimates and AI-assisted IELTS feedback.

It must not claim:

- Official CEFR certification
- Official IELTS examiner equivalence
- Guaranteed IELTS bands
- Guaranteed learning timelines
- Scientifically validated placement accuracy unless genuine validation later supports the claim

### 9. Copyright-safe content

Learning material must be:

- Original
- Properly licensed
- Public domain where appropriate
- Lawfully referenced

Commercial tests, books, audio, and course material must not be copied merely because they can be found online.

### 10. Human teachers remain important

AI should improve preparation, practice, feedback, evidence, and teacher efficiency.

It should not pretend qualified teachers are unnecessary.

## Launch philosophy

Endoora's first launch should prove that learners repeatedly use the core learning loop and receive enough value to return.

Features that introduce major safety, scientific-validity, marketplace-liquidity, copyright, financial, or technical risk may remain beta, foundation-only, feature-flagged, or Post-60.

## Success definition

Endoora succeeds when learners repeatedly complete valuable learning loops and can see credible evidence of progress while teachers can meaningfully use that evidence to support instruction.

The product must optimize for trust, retention, educational usefulness, safety, and willingness to pay rather than visual spectacle or maximum feature count.
